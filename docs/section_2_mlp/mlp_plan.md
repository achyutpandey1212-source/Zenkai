# Minimum Lovable Product (MLP)

## Product Thesis

People do not fail because they lack ambition. They fail because they carry too much cognitive load alone.

Our product exists to reduce that load by becoming a calm, trusted AI Growth Partner that understands the user, plans ahead, remembers what matters, and helps them move toward their goals without adding chaos.

---

## MLP Goal

Build the smallest version of the product that users would genuinely love using, not just try once.

A successful user should feel:
- “It understands me.”
- “I know exactly what I should do now.”
- “I feel lighter.”
- “I want to come back tomorrow.”

---

## Core Experiences

### 1) Know Me
Help the AI understand the user over time instead of relying on a one-time onboarding form.

**Includes**
- Conversational onboarding
- Goal discovery
- Long-term memory
- Behavioral learning
- User preferences
- Identity understanding (“Who are you trying to become?”)

**User feeling**
- “It understands me.”

---

### 2) Guide Me
Remove decision fatigue by proactively planning instead of waiting for instructions.

**Includes**
- Daily morning briefing
- Automatic prioritization
- AI-generated daily schedule
- Smart rescheduling
- Deadline risk detection
- Email briefings

**User feeling**
- “I don’t have to think about what comes next.”

---

### 3) Grow With Me
Turn daily conversations into long-term growth instead of temporary chats.

**Includes**
- Night reflection
- AI partner chat
- Weekly reflection
- Context-aware encouragement
- Progress narrative (“Your Story”)

**User feeling**
- “Someone is genuinely invested in my future.”

---

### 4) Keep Me Organized
Provide transparency while keeping complexity hidden.

**Includes**
- Timeline / calendar
- Tasks
- Goals
- Deadlines
- AI-created schedule

**User feeling**
- “Everything is under control.”

---

## What the MLP Must Achieve

By the end of the first week, the user should naturally experience these moments:

### Day 1
- “This AI understood what I’m trying to become.”

### Day 2
- “It already planned my day.”

### Day 5
- “It noticed something about me that I didn’t realize.”

### Day 10
- “I’m opening this before I start working.”

### Day 30
- “I trust this AI with my goals.”

---

## What We Will Not Build in V1

These are intentionally out of scope:

- Voice assistant
- WhatsApp integration
- AI avatar
- Mood tracker
- Therapy chatbot
- Habit tracker
- Pomodoro timer
- Gamification
- Social/community features
- File upload and OCR
- Gmail parsing
- Google Calendar sync
- Mobile app
- Browser notifications

### Why
These increase scope more than they increase user love for the hackathon version.

---

## Product Rules

### Rule 1
Every meaningful interaction must reduce cognitive load or deepen trust.

### Rule 2
Every emotional insight must produce a practical outcome.

### Rule 3
The AI should never just talk. It should help the user act.

### Rule 4
The app should feel calm, premium, and quiet — not noisy or overwhelming.

### Rule 5
The user should never feel like they are carrying everything in their head alone.

---

## Product Promise

Our AI does not replace your decisions. It removes unnecessary ones.

It learns who you are trying to become, proactively organizes your life, remembers what matters, and helps you consistently move toward your goals without adding more mental burden.
